   - Replace `[your.email@example.com]` with your email
   - Replace `[your-phone]` with your phone number

   - If they're focused on speed, emphasize the 21-month time savings
   - If they're cost-conscious, emphasize the $3M+ savings
   - If they're risk-averse, emphasize the 76% risk reduction

3. **Add Company-Specific Insights**:
   - Research their current tech stack
   - Understand their merchant target market
   - Know their geographic focus (India? International?)
   - Align your roadmap with their vision

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Lead with the Platform, Not the Resume**
   - Show, don't tell
   - Demo the working solution
   - Let the code speak for itself

2. **Frame as "Acqui-hire" Not "Job Application"**
   - You're offering a strategic partnership
   - They're acquiring a $3.7M asset + CTO expertise
   - This changes the conversation dynamics

3. **Be Confident About the Numbers**
   - These are conservative estimates
   - Platform value is demonstrable (78 source files)
   - Time/cost savings are industry-standard

4. **Address the "Too Good to Be True" Concern**
   - Acknowledge it's an unusual offer
   - Explain you built this for strategic positioning
   - Show the code, documentation, and architecture
   - Offer thorough due diligence

5. **Have a Clear Ask**
   - Don't be vague about next steps
   - Propose specific actions (demo, technical review, etc.)
   - Timeline-driven (30 days to decision)
